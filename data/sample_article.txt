Apple Reports Fiscal Q4 Earnings Beat as Services Revenue Reaches All-Time High

Apple Inc. ($AAPL) delivered better-than-expected quarterly financial results, posting revenue of $89.5 billion, which exceeded consensus analyst estimates by 2.4%. Gross margins expanded by 110 basis points to 45.2%, bolstered by record customer demand for Cloud and Services subscriptions.

Chief Executive Tim Cook highlighted momentum across key international markets, while the board of directors declared a cash dividend of $0.24 per share and authorized an increase in the ongoing share repurchase program.

In contrast, semiconductor supplier Qualcomm ($QCOM) issued cautious forward guidance, citing smartphone inventory overhang in Asian retail channels and flagging lower royalty revenue expectations for the coming quarter.

Despite macro headwinds and currency fluctuations, Apple reiterated its long-term commitment to hardware innovation and silicon supply chain investments.
